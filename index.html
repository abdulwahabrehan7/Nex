HARDWARE & WORKSTATIONS

Think beyond the error message.

For desktop and workstation issues, start with observable symptoms and safe physical checks. Follow company ESD, warranty and change-control procedures before opening equipment.

PC does not power on

Confirm wall power, power cable, PSU switch and dock/power adapter. Check for LEDs or fan activity. If there is no response, isolate the outlet and adapter where safe. Escalate suspected PSU or board faults.

Overheating or loud fans

Check airflow, blocked vents, dust build-up and fan operation. Review temperatures using an approved monitoring utility. Avoid stress tests on unstable or overheating systems; clean only under approved procedures.

Display or peripheral issue

Check monitor power/input, cable seating, dock connection and alternate port. Test a known-good cable or monitor, then review display settings and device drivers.

After hardware upgrade

Verify component compatibility, seating, power connectors, BIOS recognition and device drivers. Run an appropriate diagnostic and document the part/model and test outcome.

SERVICE DESK PRACTICE

Write better ticket notes.

Clear notes help the next technician understand what happened without repeating the same checks. Use this template for practice or adapt it to your organisation's ITSM process.

Good ticket habits

  • • Record exact error messages where possible.
  • • Include device name, user impact and time observed.
  • • Never include passwords or authentication codes.
  • • Record actions and results, not assumptions.
